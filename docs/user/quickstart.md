# Arrhythmia quick start

1. **Back up.** Turn the unit off, take out the microSD card and copy it to your computer.
2. **Download** Novation's firmware 2.0.1: <https://components.novationmusic.com/api/v2/firmwares/169/file>.
3. **Put the card back.** Close Novation Components and any other music app. Connect the unit by USB and turn it on.
4. **Open** <https://llcoolkm.github.io/arrhythmia/web/> in Chrome. Press **Connect MIDI**, choose the downloaded
   file, press **Install**.
5. **Press Play** on the unit when it flashes. Wait about half a minute; the unit restarts.
6. **Play** some pads on track 8: you hear the synth.

Problems, the way back to stock and everything else: [install guide](install.md).
